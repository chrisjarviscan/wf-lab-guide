# Writing skills

Both writing checks are built into the current Lab Guide. No extra installation for Lab 2.
They preserve your approved organizational voice, source facts and quotations.

## Use them outside the lab

Download these two skills:

1. [AI Syntax Avoidance](download/ai-syntax-avoidance.zip): removes machine-like phrasing.
2. [Extended craft review](download/ai-syntax-avoidance-extended.zip): checks flow, empathy and structure.

Then, where Claude offers **Skills → Upload**, upload both ZIPs and enable them. Keep your work chat.
Use both; the extended review runs after the base check. If controls are unavailable, continue with the guide.
The repository plugin includes both skills too. Update through the controls your account actually offers.

Give Claude one permitted paragraph. Say **“Show me before and after.”**
Compare the original and proposed revision. Claude explains one change; you judge the result.
A strong paragraph may need no changes. For findings without edits, say **“Audit only.”**
Claude keeps facts intact and asks before changing existing files. It does not copy Chris's voice onto your writing.
Style patterns are editing signals, not proof that AI wrote something. Your judgment and fact-check still matter.
