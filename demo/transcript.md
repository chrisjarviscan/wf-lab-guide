# Practice transcript: a newsletter proposal

Everything in this transcript is fictional. It contains no real participant, client, donor or volunteer information. Paragraph labels are source references; this transcript has no timestamps.

**Paragraph 1.** We need our board to decide whether to pilot a monthly newsletter. At present, the communications team sends six separate email updates each month. We want to make our program updates easier for the board to follow.

**Paragraph 2.** The proposal is to combine those updates into one monthly newsletter. We have not yet checked whether all six updates fit that format. A shorter email and a two-page PDF are two design options we could compare.

**Paragraph 3.** We agreed that the communications team will prepare those two layout options for the next meeting. Neither design is approved. We did not approve a budget or launch date, and we did not set a deadline for preparing the options.

**Paragraph 4.** The pilot decision remains open. At the next meeting, we need the board to confirm the audience, choose a design direction and decide whether the team should test the newsletter. For now, the next action is to prepare the two options, not launch a newsletter.
