# Lab Guide for Applied AI Labs for Nonprofits

Ask your own Claude about the labs: the dates, what to bring, a kit's steps, the homework, the rules. The Lab Guide answers from the program's participant page and lab kits, and it puts its version and date at the top of every answer, so you can see which version answered.

Applied AI Labs for Nonprofits is run by RW Institute and funded by Wells Fargo Philanthropy and Community Impact (PCI). The Lab Guide runs inside your own Claude account. Your questions go to Claude, the same as any other chat. Adding the guide costs no money; each question uses a little of your Claude usage, like any chat.

## Add it to your Claude

Use the first way that works for you.

### 1. On a paid Claude plan: add the plugin

Works on Pro and Max, and on Team or Enterprise seats if your organization lets members add plugins.

1. In Claude, open **Customize** in the left sidebar, then the **Plugins** tab.
2. Click **Add**, then **Add marketplace**, then **Add from a repository**.
3. Enter `chrisjarviscan/wf-lab-guide`.
4. Find **wf-lab-guide** and add it.
5. Start a new chat, type `/`, choose **lab-guide**, and then type your question. Questions that mention the labs often work without it, but choosing the Lab Guide first makes sure it answers.

### 2. On a free plan, or a work seat without plugins: upload the skill

1. Download [lab-guide.zip](https://github.com/chrisjarviscan/wf-lab-guide/raw/main/download/lab-guide.zip).
2. On a personal plan, go to **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, and switch on **Code execution and file creation**. On a Team seat, your organization switches this on.
3. Go to **Customize**, then **Skills**. Click the **+** button, then **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**, and choose `lab-guide.zip`.
4. Check that the Lab Guide is switched on in your skills list. Then, in a new chat, type `/`, choose **lab-guide**, and type your question.

When the guide is updated, download the ZIP again and upload it to replace the old one. Compare the version at the top of an answer with the version at the top of this page.

### 3. Anywhere else: copy one prompt

On a phone, or where you can't add skills, use the [copy-prompt](download/copy-prompt.md). It knows only the dates, the times and the main rules.

## Good to know

- Ask in a fresh chat, outside your AI-Labs Project, so older copies of the kits don't mix in.
- For Claude's own settings and screens, the guide goes by Anthropic's help pages, because they change often.
- If an answer looks wrong, email Nichole Giller at nichole@realizedworth.com and mention the version it showed.
- The guide knows nothing about other participants and never asks for anything that doesn't go into Claude.
